# How to cite

## The MEPS data

AHRQ asks that users cite AHRQ and the Medical Expenditure Panel Survey as the
data source. For the four files used here, for example:

- Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality. *Medical Expenditure Panel
  Survey: MEPS HC-251, 2023 Full Year Consolidated Data File.* Rockville, MD:
  AHRQ; August 2025. https://meps.ahrq.gov/mepsweb/data_stats/download_data_files_detail.jsp?cboPufNumber=HC-251
- Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality. *Medical Expenditure Panel
  Survey: MEPS HC-249, 2023 Medical Conditions.* Rockville, MD: AHRQ; 2025.
- Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality. *Medical Expenditure Panel
  Survey: MEPS HC-248D, 2023 Hospital Inpatient Stays.* Rockville, MD: AHRQ; 2025.
- Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality. *Medical Expenditure Panel
  Survey: MEPS HC-252, Panel 27 Longitudinal Data File (2022–2023).*
  Rockville, MD: AHRQ; September 2025.

(The release month for each file is on its page; check it for HC-249 and
HC-248D.)

## Methods used in the examples

- Plasmode simulation: Franklin, J. M., S. Schneeweiss, J. M. Polinski and
  J. A. Rassen (2014). Plasmode simulation for the evaluation of
  pharmacoepidemiologic methods in complex healthcare databases.
  *Computational Statistics & Data Analysis* 72, 219–226.
- Planning from a pilot's SD: Browne, R. H. (1995). On the use of a pilot
  sample for sample size determination. *Statistics in Medicine* 14(17),
  1933–1940; Bonett, D. G. (2006). Approximate confidence interval for
  standard deviation of nonnormal distributions. *Computational Statistics &
  Data Analysis* 50, 775–782.
- BCa intervals: Efron, B. (1987). Better bootstrap confidence intervals.
  *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 82(397), 171–185.
- Survey inference in R: Lumley, T. (2004). Analysis of complex survey
  samples. *Journal of Statistical Software* 9(8), 1–19. The PSU bootstrap:
  Rao, J. N. K. and C. F. J. Wu (1988). Resampling inference with complex
  survey data. *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 83(401),
  231–241.
